A Message from the Chairman

Turning Field Data into Real-World Impact

Every day, across agriculture and other essential industries, critical decisions are made with limited visibility into what is actually happening in the field. Valuable information is often unavailable, fragmented or simply lost.

At NexVia Konect, we believe this must change.

Our vision is to bridge the gap between physical operations and digital intelligence by making trusted, actionable field data accessible, even in environments where connectivity, energy and infrastructure are limited.

We are starting in Rwanda with SmartCoffee, our first real-world application. Through this initiative, we have demonstrated the technical foundations of an end-to-end IoT platform that connects physical sensors to secure cloud services, combining data acquisition, cellular connectivity, geolocation, historical storage and remote monitoring.

But our ambition extends far beyond coffee.

We are building a reusable technology platform designed to support multiple industries, from agriculture and water management to livestock, transport and environmental monitoring. Our long-term goal is to transform reliable field data into increasingly valuable services, including predictive analytics and artificial intelligence.

We also recognize that technology alone does not create lasting value. Our next priority is to work closely with field partners and customers to demonstrate operational reliability, measurable economic benefits and a sustainable business model.

We are building NexVia Konect with a clear conviction: meaningful innovation begins with real-world problems, grows through trusted partnerships and succeeds when it delivers tangible value.

Our journey starts with SmartCoffee. Our vision is much bigger.

One platform. Multiple industries. Growing intelligence.

Alexandre BARANCIRA

Chairman & Co-Founder
